The Story

Who is Qev?

Just a guy who fell in love with music

I remember wanting to be a rapper after the J.Lo and Lil Wayne song 'I'm Into You'. I wrote Lil Wayne's verse down and would rap it to everybody in school. That led me to reciting poems, then writing my own lyrics and rapping them.

I was in class 5 or 6 at the time. I'm now 25 and turning 26 on October 28, 2026.

Where are you from?

I'm Kenyan. I've lived most of my life in Juja, but my home where my parents are is somewhere in Komothai called Githuya in Kiambu County, Githunguri Constituency.

What kind of music do you make?

I rap about what I feel. I try to use my experiences to tell stories. Only telling my truth. I use music as my therapy and as a way of expressing myself.

What is Keljey?

It's a musical duo of two guys, Kelvin and James, who through the love of music try to tell their stories in rap music. We started releasing music as a duo around 2018.

What are you trying to build?

An entertainment empire. Just make music with talented people and try to reach and uplift as many people in the world as possible. Sky is the limit. And of course make a lot of money along the way to help my family and my friends.
