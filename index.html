The Duo

Keljey

Kelvin & James — Telling stories through rap since 2018

Keljey is a musical duo consisting of Kelvin and James. They started releasing music as a duo around 2018 with the vision of using rap and music to tell their stories.

The larger vision is to build an entertainment empire, work with talented people, reach and uplift people around the world, and make enough money to help family and friends.

"Through the love of music, we try to tell our stories in rap. Sky is the limit."
— Keljey
